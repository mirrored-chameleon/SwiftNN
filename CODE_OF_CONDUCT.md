# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
SwiftNN community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community
* Keeping discussions constructive and focused on SwiftNN
* Respecting contributors with different levels of technical experience
* Providing clear and constructive feedback on code, documentation, issues,
  pull requests, and proposals
* Being patient with contributors and maintainers, recognizing that SwiftNN
  is an open-source project and responses may not always be immediate

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
  of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission
* Deliberately disrupting discussions, issues, pull requests, or other
  community spaces
* Repeatedly submitting irrelevant or intentionally harmful contributions
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate or harmful
  in a community setting

## Pull Requests and Contributions

SwiftNN is built through contributions from its community. Pull requests are
encouraged, and contributors of all experience levels are welcome to suggest
improvements.

When submitting a pull request, contributors should make a reasonable effort
to ensure that their changes:

* Solve the problem or add the feature they are intended to address
* Contain clear, readable, and maintainable code
* Work as intended and do not knowingly introduce broken functionality
* Include appropriate tests when practical
* Do not unnecessarily break or remove existing SwiftNN functionality
* Do not introduce unrelated changes that make the pull request harder to
  review
* Follow the existing structure and conventions of the project where
  reasonably possible

Pull requests should aim to **add to and improve SwiftNN, not destroy or
needlessly destabilize it**. Experimental ideas and significant changes are
welcome, but contributors should clearly explain their purpose, limitations,
and potential impact on the project.

A pull request does not need to be perfect to be valuable. Maintainers may
request changes, suggest alternatives, or help contributors improve their
work before it is merged.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action
in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, pull requests, and other
contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct or that could
unreasonably harm the project or its community. When appropriate, reasons for
moderation decisions will be communicated.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including the SwiftNN
GitHub repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces
officially associated with the project.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the SwiftNN
community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include
using an official e-mail address, posting via an official social media account,
or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at
davyn.monagle@icloud.com.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as reasonably possible.

Community leaders will respect the privacy and security of the reporter of
any incident and will share information only when reasonably necessary to
address the report.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining
the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct.
The response may depend on the severity and circumstances of the violation.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning from community leaders, providing
clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the
behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested where
appropriate.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of
actions.

**Consequence:** A warning explaining the violation and the consequences of
continued behavior. Continued violations may result in a temporary or
permanent ban from the community.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from community spaces for a specified period
of time. Further violations during or after the ban may lead to a permanent
ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community
standards, sustained harassment of an individual, serious aggression toward
or disparagement of classes of individuals, or other behavior that poses a
significant risk to the community.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from public interaction within the SwiftNN
community.
